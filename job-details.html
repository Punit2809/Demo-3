Job Description

We're looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join the platform team at Northwind. You'll design, build and scale backend services that power our core products, collaborating closely with product, design and infrastructure teams.

This is a high-impact role with real ownership — you'll influence architecture, mentor engineers and help set the technical direction of the team.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and implement scalable backend services using Java and Spring Boot.
  • Build and maintain RESTful APIs consumed by web and mobile clients.
  • Deploy and operate services on AWS with CI/CD best practices.
  • Participate in code reviews and mentor junior engineers.
  • Collaborate with product managers to define and deliver new features.
  • Contribute to architecture decisions and technical roadmaps.

Requirements

  • 5+ years of professional software engineering experience.
  • Strong proficiency in Java and the Spring ecosystem.
  • Experience designing microservices and distributed systems.
  • Hands-on experience with AWS (EC2, S3, Lambda, RDS).
  • Solid understanding of relational databases and SQL.
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary with performance bonus.
  • Comprehensive health, dental and vision coverage.
  • 401(k) with company match.
  • Flexible remote-first working arrangements.
  • Generous paid time off and learning budget.
